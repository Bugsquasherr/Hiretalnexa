About Hiretalnexa

An IT staffing and recruitment consultancy that helps companies hire, and helps candidates get hired.

Who we are

Hiretalnexa (also written Hire Talnexa) is an IT staffing and recruitment consultancy. We help businesses hire skilled professionals on contract, permanent and contract-to-hire terms, and we help job seekers find genuine openings at no cost to them.

Our aim is simple: make hiring clear and honest. Companies get relevant, screened candidates and regular updates. Candidates get fair guidance and a straightforward process.

What we do

Flexible hiring models built around your project, budget and timeline.

Contract staffing

Skilled professionals for short or long projects.

Permanent recruitment

Full-time hires matched to your technical needs and your company culture.

Contract-to-hire

Work with a candidate first, then convert them to a permanent employee when you are confident.

IT consultancy

Guidance on team structure, hiring plans and technology choices to help you build the right team.
